Peekahed – How to Delete Your Account

This page explains how you can request deletion of your Peekahed account and associated data.

What will be deleted

  • Your Peekahed account (Google sign‑in identity used in the app).
  • Your uploaded photos and comments, including their location metadata.
  • Any in‑app feedback or reports linked to your user ID.

Note: Some data may be retained for a short period in backups or logs as required for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations, but will no longer be used in the app.

How to request deletion

To delete your account, please send an email to:

peekahed@gmail.com

Include the following information:

  • Subject: Peekahed – Account Deletion Request
  • The Google account (email address) you use to sign in to Peekahed.
  • A short confirmation that you want your account and data deleted.

We will process your request and delete your account and associated data as soon as reasonably possible, typically within 30 days.

In‑app deletion (if available)

If a direct “Delete account” option is added inside the app in a future update, you will be able to request deletion from within the app’s profile/settings section. Until then, please use the email method described above.

Contact

If you have any questions about account deletion or data handling, you can contact us at peekahed@gmail.com.
