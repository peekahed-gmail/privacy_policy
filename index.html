Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 6, 2025

Peekahed is a community photo and comment app that groups content by location for easier viewing. This policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Account: Email and profile information through sign in (e.g., Google Sign-In).
  • Content: Photos, comments, and optional screenshots you upload.
  • Location: Device location at the time of upload (when permitted), used for grouping on the map.
  • Usage: Basic diagnostics (errors) to improve app reliability.

How We Use Information

  • Show nearby photos/comments in clusters on the map.
  • Apply AI processing to obscure faces or text where necessary before upload.
  • Enable feedback and support by saving your submissions in a secure database.

Data Sharing

  • Photos and comments may be visible to other users near the location.
  • We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by service providers (e.g., Firebase) to deliver app functionality.

Retention and Deletion

  • Content may be retained for a limited time and auto-deleted per lifecycle rules (e.g., two days), or earlier if you delete it.
  • You can request deletion of your account data by contacting support.

Your Choices

  • Permissions: You can deny camera/location permissions; certain features may not work without them.
  • Visibility: Avoid uploading sensitive personal information. Use feedback to report issues.

Security

We use industry-standard practices and Firebase security rules to protect data. No system is 100% secure.
